# Connection Protection and Rebooking — NS-CONN-2026

Effective January 1, 2026. Owner: Customer Operations. This fictional Northstar Air policy covers domestic protected connections. It describes recovery eligibility; an eligible passenger still needs a confirmed seat before travel is arranged.

## 1. Protected connections
A missed connection and a refund are separate decisions. Use this policy for replacement travel and NS-REF-2026 for refunds. Hotel assistance is assessed separately under NS-CARE-2026.

### 1.1 Covered transportation
A protected itinerary has at least two domestic Northstar-operated flights on the same ticket and reservation, sold as one continuous journey. The first flight must be disrupted before the onward flight's boarding cutoff. Separate tickets, codeshares, interline travel and international itineraries are outside this policy; refer those passengers to a specialist.

### 1.2 Confirming a missed connection
Compare actual inbound gate arrival, required transfer time and the onward boarding cutoff. A delayed inbound flight does not prove a missed connection if onward boarding remains open. Missing or contradictory operational times require review.

## 2. Rebooking and passenger choice
Rebooking eligibility, an available offer and a confirmed ticket are different stages. Staff must explain which stage has actually been reached.

### 2.1 No-additional-fare rebooking
For a verified protected missed connection, Northstar offers the earliest available Northstar-operated itinerary to the original destination at no additional fare. This applies to weather and other uncontrollable causes as well as mechanical, crew and airline-operations disruptions. Seat availability must be confirmed; no particular departure time is guaranteed.

### 2.2 Available alternatives
Search feasible same-day service, later connections and then next-day travel, considering the passenger's documented needs. A search result or temporary seat hold is not a confirmed reservation. External-carrier transfers require separate approval.

### 2.3 Passenger decision
Record an offer as OFFERED, ACCEPTED, DECLINED or EXPIRED, including its timestamp and notification channel. Expiry or silence does not prove acceptance. A passenger may decline an alternative without losing an otherwise qualifying refund option.

### 2.4 Boarding feasibility
An aircraft still at the gate may already be closed for boarding. Use recorded boarding events and realistic transfer constraints, rather than scheduled departure alone. Escalate conflicting timestamps instead of guessing.

### 2.5 Offer versus confirmed booking
A text offering another flight does not mean the passenger is booked. Distinguish SEARCHED, OFFERED, ACCEPTED, TICKETED and FLOWN. Confirmation requires an actual ticketed reservation. If no seats are available, record NO_CURRENT_INVENTORY: recovery eligibility remains, but transport is not confirmed.

### 2.6 Changed routing
A different airport, additional connection or substantial arrival change may affect refund eligibility. Check accessibility needs before presenting a routing as suitable. Fare price alone does not establish suitability.

### 2.7 Required information
Record the original ticket, affected segments, boarding and arrival times, available alternatives, passenger choice and applicable policy version. An unresolved cause does not prevent protected rebooking; cause matters separately for passenger care.

### 2.8 Conflicting messages
A gate agent, app and phone agent may show different inventory snapshots. Identify the current offer and whether a ticket exists. Multiple messages are not multiple confirmed bookings. Tell the passenger which offer replaced an earlier proposal.

## 3. Refund alternative
A missed connection does not automatically create a refund. Check cancellation or significant itinerary change and the passenger's decision under NS-REF-2026. Completed replacement travel generally prevents a full refund for transportation used.

## 4. Passenger care
A free rebooking does not automatically include meals or a hotel. Assess approved cause, waiting time, overnight necessity and residence under NS-CARE-2026.

## 5. Escalation
When boarding evidence, ticket linkage or inventory is missing, explain what needs verification. Never claim that a flight has been booked or that compensation has been paid without confirmation.

## 6. Scope examples
A weather-delayed passenger on one protected Northstar ticket may qualify for no-additional-fare rebooking. A traveler on two separately purchased tickets is outside protected-connection coverage and needs specialist review. Neither example alone determines hotel or refund eligibility.
