# Passenger Care and Hotels — NS-CARE-2025

Effective January 1, 2025. Owner: Customer Experience Operations. This edition defines Northstar Air's fictional domestic passenger-care commitments for the 2025 program year. Meals and accommodation are assessed separately from ticket refunds and replacement travel.

## 1. Coverage
Assistance applies to an affected passenger at a U.S. airport who holds a valid Northstar ticket. Staff must confirm the operational cause, waiting period and applicable accommodation conditions before offering a standard benefit.

### 1.1 Covered passengers
The passenger must be unable to continue on the originally scheduled Northstar service because of a disruption. A traveler seeking a hotel must be away from their documented home metropolitan area. The departure airport and billing address are not substitutes for verified residence.

### 1.2 Eligible controllable causes
For this edition, eligible controllable causes are final confirmed MECHANICAL and AIRLINE_OPERATIONS events. Crew shortages and rotation changes are a separate category with narrower hotel assistance. An informal gate comment or passenger assumption is not an approved cause finding.

## 2. Basic assistance
Explain flight status, feasible alternatives and any information still needed. Never promise a particular flight, room or payment before fulfillment is confirmed.

### 2.1 Communication and rebooking
Search for available onward travel under the connection-protection rules effective for the journey. A proposed itinerary remains an offer until accepted and ticketed. Hotel eligibility does not follow automatically from a replacement-flight offer.

### 2.2 Meal vouchers
After three consecutive hours of waiting caused by an eligible controllable event in §1.2, provide a standard meal voucher. Weather and external-ATC delays are excluded. Exceptional goodwill assistance may be authorized separately and must not be described as a routine entitlement.

## 3. Overnight hotels
A delayed arrival alone is insufficient for accommodation. Confirm the cause, overnight necessity and home-metro conditions together.

### 3.1 Standard hotel rule
Provide an approved hotel when a verified MECHANICAL or AIRLINE_OPERATIONS disruption makes an overnight stay necessary at the intermediate airport and the passenger is away from home. Authorized staff must issue the voucher or approve reimbursement in writing.

### 3.2 Crew-related exclusion
A final CREW_OPERATIONAL cause does not qualify for the standard overnight-hotel commitment in this edition. Crew scheduling problems, reserve shortages and rotation changes are excluded from this routine hotel benefit, even when attributable to Northstar. A duty manager may authorize discretionary assistance, but that is a courtesy rather than an entitlement.

### 3.3 Weather and unresolved causes
Weather and external air-traffic restrictions do not qualify for ordinary hotel assistance. Staff may provide local hotel information without promising airline payment. If the final cause is UNKNOWN or MIXED_PENDING, retain the case for review instead of assuming an exclusion.

### 3.4 Overnight and home-city restrictions
The passenger must need an overnight stay into the following calendar day because feasible onward travel is unavailable. A traveler stranded in their own home metropolitan area does not qualify for a standard room. Missing or conflicting residence information requires verification.

### 3.5 Courtesy exceptions
Crew-related hotel requests may be referred to a duty manager for an individually approved COURTESY booking. Record the approval and explain that this exception does not change the standard crew exclusion. Accessibility or safety needs require trained human review; a single courtesy booking does not establish general eligibility.

### 3.6 Date of service
Apply the edition in force when the disruption occurred, rather than the date on which an appeal or expense claim is reviewed. Retain the event date and applicable policy reference with the decision. Ask for the date when it is missing and materially affects the rule.

## 4. Authorization and records
Keep eligibility and actual fulfillment separate. A policy-based entitlement is not proof that a room was reserved or an expense paid.

### 4.1 Independent bookings
Before self-booking, obtain written authorization identifying the approved dates and maximum reimbursable rate. A receipt submitted afterwards or an informal verbal suggestion alone does not establish reimbursement approval.

### 4.2 Decision records
Retain the ticket, affected flight, approved cause, onward itinerary, residence finding, voucher or written authorization and agent approval. Explain any missing facts. Do not describe an offer as a completed booking.

## 5. Examples
For an overnight traveler away from home, a confirmed mechanical failure can qualify for the standard hotel. A confirmed crew scheduling problem does not qualify under this edition, though a manager may approve a courtesy. Weather is excluded, and an unresolved cause remains under review.

## 6. Related benefits
Use the applicable connection policy for replacement travel and the applicable refund policy for unused transportation. A hotel decision alone does not determine whether a passenger qualifies for rebooking or a refund.
