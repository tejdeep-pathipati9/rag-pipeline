# Meals and Overnight Hotels — NS-CARE-2026

Effective January 1, 2026. Owner: Customer Experience Operations. These are fictional Northstar Air service commitments for disrupted domestic travel, not universal legal rights. Assess meals, hotels, rebooking and refunds separately.

## 1. Coverage and definitions
Use the policy effective on the disruption date. A passenger asking about today's benefits should receive the current rule, while a historical claim uses the edition in force at that time.

### 1.1 Covered passengers
The passenger must hold a valid disrupted Northstar itinerary and be present at the affected airport. Protected rebooking is governed separately by NS-CONN-2026.

### 1.2 Airline-controlled causes and home metro
MECHANICAL, CREW_OPERATIONAL and AIRLINE_OPERATIONS are airline-controlled causes. WEATHER and ATC_EXTERNAL are uncontrollable. UNKNOWN and MIXED_PENDING are unresolved. Home metro means the passenger's verified metropolitan area of residence, not their payment address or departure airport.

## 2. Basic assistance
Explain the flight status, feasible recovery options and any information still needed. Discretionary assistance must be recorded as COURTESY rather than a standard entitlement. A service offer does not certify that it was delivered.

## 3. Meal vouchers
Meal eligibility depends on the waiting time and approved cause, independently of hotel eligibility.

### 3.1 Three-hour waiting threshold
Provide a meal voucher after at least three consecutive hours of waiting for onward travel due to a final approved airline-controlled cause. Mechanical, crew and airline-operations disruptions can qualify. Do not count the same waiting interval twice when offers change.

### 3.2 Meal exclusions
Weather-only and external air-traffic-control delays do not qualify for standard meal vouchers. If the cause is unconfirmed, meal eligibility requires review. Staff may separately authorize courtesy assistance.

## 4. Overnight accommodation
Determine eligibility before arranging or reimbursing a room. An overnight delay alone is insufficient.

### 4.1 Primary hotel eligibility
Northstar provides an approved hotel when all four conditions hold: a valid disrupted Northstar itinerary; a final approved airline-controlled cause; onward travel requiring an overnight wait at the affected airport; and a passenger away from their verified home metro area. All conditions are required together.

### 4.2 Overnight necessity
An overnight wait means no reasonable onward option can be completed without staying at the recovery station into the following calendar day. Use feasible flight options and boarding constraints. A late departure or device date change alone does not establish overnight necessity.

### 4.3 Home-city restriction
A passenger stranded at an airport within their own home metro area is not eligible for the standard hotel. This applies even if their trip began elsewhere. Missing residence information requires review.

### 4.4 Weather, external ATC and unresolved causes
Weather and external ATC disruptions do not qualify for standard hotel coverage. UNKNOWN, MIXED_PENDING or an investigation still in progress means NEEDS_REVIEW; do not promise payment or assume the cause is uncontrollable.

### 4.5 Crew scheduling coverage
A finalized CREW_OPERATIONAL disruption is included in current hotel coverage. A crew shortage or rotation problem can qualify when the overnight, away-from-home and valid-itinerary conditions in §4.1 also hold. This inclusion took effect January 1, 2026.

### 4.6 Self-booked hotels and written authorization
Northstar normally issues an approved hotel voucher. Before booking independently, obtain written authorization stating the maximum reimbursable rate and approved dates. A receipt or verbal suggestion alone does not establish reimbursement approval.

### 4.7 Approved cause controls
The latest authorized, approved final investigation controls eligibility, even if a gate announcement initially said weather. A later unapproved draft cannot override an approved finding. Consult NS-CAUSE-2026 for investigation states.

### 4.8 Eligibility versus fulfillment
Full partner hotels do not remove eligibility. Record fulfillment separately as NOT_REQUESTED, SEARCHING, VOUCHER_AUTHORIZED, VOUCHER_ISSUED, RECEIPT_PENDING or CLOSED. Authorization is not proof of an issued voucher or booked room. Arrange alternative approved accommodation or escalate capacity problems.

### 4.9 Residence verification
Do not substitute billing address, nationality, telephone area code or trip origin for verified residence. Conflicting residence sources require authorized verification before deciding the home-metro condition.

## 5. Review and communication
Explain which eligibility conditions hold and which facts are missing. Escalate accessibility or safety needs to trained staff. Never state that a room is reserved, an expense reimbursed or a benefit delivered without fulfillment evidence. Refund options remain governed by NS-REF-2026.
